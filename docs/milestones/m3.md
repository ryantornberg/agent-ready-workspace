# M3, the reading layer, the safety machinery and the kit: milestone record

The record of the third milestone, written as its slices finish. The plan is [the M3 hand-off plan](../plans/m3-reading-layer-machinery-and-kit.md).

**In short:** sub-batch A is built and recorded, and slices C1, C2 and C7 are merged. The eval harness is generalized, every page names its kind and opens in one order, rules carry enforcement tags, and every playbook and how-to says whether it is proven. R1 gave 41, 18 and 0 of 45 runs passing every grader, within 3 of M2's 40, with no re-record. Sub-batch B, slices C3 to C6, C8 and C9, slice Z and the outside checks are still to come.
