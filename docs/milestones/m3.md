# M3, the reading layer, the safety machinery and the kit: milestone record

The record of the third milestone, written as its slices finish. The plan is [the M3 hand-off plan](../plans/m3-reading-layer-machinery-and-kit.md).

**In short:** sub-batch A is built and recorded, and slices B1, C1, C2, C7 and C3 are merged. The eval harness is generalized, every page names its kind and opens in one order, rules carry enforcement tags, and every playbook and how-to says whether it is proven. R1 gave 41, 18 and 0 of 45 runs passing every grader, within 3 of M2's 40, with no re-record. Sub-batch B's port ledger (B0) maps all 200 catalog items it must account for. B1's instruction review is merged: a guidance change names its review record in a commit trailer, checked at the commit, the push to main and in CI, and its first run left a record for each page sub-batch A wrote. B2 wrote three of its five batches: twenty procedure, routing and review pages, among them the playbook catalog, the how-to index and the review orchestrator. The stop rule stopped it after each, and it waits for the maintainer's decision on how the rule gates batch 4. C4 is merged: `python3 ws.py merge` merges on GitHub with a merge commit once CI passed on the head commit, and merged its own pull request; a push from the main working copy is refused while another session holds it. C5 confirmed that both agent tools offer the after-edit and stop events, then stopped before building: it is too large for one pull request, and a six-way split waits for the maintainer's yes. The rest of B2, slices B3 to B7, C5, C6, C8 and C9, slice Z and the outside checks are still to come.
