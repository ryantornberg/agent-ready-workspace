# Red team review: docs/plans/m4-fail-closed-guards-review-loop-and-evals.md

A thirteenth fresh red team's attack on M4's draft hand-off plan, checking the claims its decisions rest on against the repository.

**In short:** holds with fixes, 5 findings. The plan reviews H0b and H3's work after the eval runs that a review fix would spoil, and slice Z must edit pages under a proof attempt.

The red team read the plan, the round 12 record and the earlier rounds' findings, the red team review page, and the sources the plan names, at the commit above, with a clean tree. It also read `parts/eval_record.py`, `parts/proof.py`, `parts/wrappers.py`, `parts/plan_budgets.py`, `parts/rules.py`, `parts/no_stage_all.py`, `evals/grade.py`, the hashing, baseline and one-time code in `evals/run.py`, `evals/arms.json`, `evals/one-time.json`, every case under `evals/cases/`, every stored `record.json` under `evals/runs/`, the wrappers' descriptions under `plugin/skills/`, the manifest's `direct_push`, `wrappers`, `loaded_size` and `review_trailer` entries, and `.github/workflows/ci.yml`. It ran `python3 ws.py check`, the plan's two V8 greps, `git log` counts on `origin/main` for the ten pages, and `git grep` for links into `docs/parts.md` and `docs/changing-the-machinery.md`. It ran no hook attack, and no agent, eval or selection run.

## Verdict

**Holds with fixes.** The core holds: one literal-word rule in the parser, allow lists for git settings and `gh` writes, grading files out of the recording's hash, staged eval budgets, and a review mark bound to dispatched sessions. The arithmetic of every budget adds up, and H0a can leave `run.py` alone, as it claims. But the plan never puts a review before the eval runs it can spoil, so a finding in H0b's harness after R3, or in an H3 case after its baseline, either stops the plan or breaks criterion 30 (finding 1). Round 8's fix keeps two pointer headings on pages under a proof attempt until slice Z, and Z's removal of them, like a cut of the delegating section, edits a page criterion 30 freezes and, once proven, the eval gate holds, with no recording budgeted (finding 2). A cut also forces edits to other wrappers' descriptions, which the selection budget does not price (finding 3). Each has a local fix.

## What survives

- **Every budget sum adds up.** Eval runs: 135 + 400 + 660 + 45 + 45 = 1,285, and 1,420 with every re-record. All ten pages recorded: 1,945 and 2,080. Upkeep with five pages proven: 5 × 132 + 135 = 795. Selection runs: 12 + 120 + 24 + 72 + 75 + 40 = 343, so 57 are left under 400. Review sessions: 18 + 8 + 15 + 52 + 24 + 55 + 15 + 22 + 19 = 228. Red team findings: 114 over twelve rounds. Verified, by adding.
- **H0a can leave `run.py` and `proofkeys.py` alone.** `run.py`'s `covered()` builds its hash by calling `eval_record.covered_files` and `eval_record.guidance_hash`, so moving grading files out of the hash in `eval_record` moves them for the runner too. Verified, by reading `evals/run.py`.
- **The fallback's exposure is narrow.** Only the three fix-a-bug cases grade through a hook (`no_stage_all`), so only they and their one-time records can change grade when G2 changes the parser. Debugging's and red team review's cases have no hook grader. Verified, by reading each case's graders.
- **Criterion 20's anchor numbers match the records.** The three fix-a-bug records' guided arms pass 14, 15 and 13 of 15, which is 42 of 45 pooled. Verified, by reading the records.
- **The churn figures hold.** On `origin/main`, `parts.md` changed in 55 commits and `changing-the-machinery.md` in 22, and the other pages in 1 to 6. Verified, by `git log`.
- **New pages at M6 pass the `proof` check.** It fails a `prove-by` newly set to a milestone in `building`, and M6 is not. A move to M6 is licensed by a failed attempt added while M4 was being built. Verified, by reading `parts/proof.py`.
- **The plan passes its own budgets check.** `python3 ws.py check` exits 0 on this tree, `plan_budgets` included. Verified, by running it.
- **H3's own cases do not hash the frozen contract, if they follow debugging's.** Debugging's and red team review's guided arms get a contract copy under `evals/proof/`, not `AGENTS.md` or `INDEX.md`, so Z's edits to those two do not stale them. Verified, by reading both cases' arms. Inferred: that H3's new cases follow the same form, since the plan says each attempt "runs as M3's slice B7 did" but does not require it.
