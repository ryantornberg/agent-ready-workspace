# Red team review: docs/plans/m4-fail-closed-guards-review-loop-and-evals.md

A seventh fresh red team's attack on M4's draft hand-off plan, checking the claims its decisions rest on against the repository.

**In short:** holds with fixes, 7 findings. H3's new two-line baseline cannot run, since `run.py --baseline` runs the bare arm alone and is frozen before H3. Criterion 30 forbids the cuts and table moves H3 itself plans.

The red team read the plan, the six earlier records and the sources the plan names, at the commit above. It read `evals/run.py`'s `plan_runs`, `covered` and `main`, `parts/eval_record.py`'s `covered_files`, `judge`, `pooled` and `fisher_greater`, `evals/arms.json`, every case under `evals/cases/`, `parts/stop_gate.py`, `parts/wrappers.py`, `wslib/parts.py`'s `NEEDS` and `TOP_LEVEL`, `kit/kit.json`, `.githooks/`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, the review orchestrator's steps and dimensions, and the hand-off plan template. It ran `python3 ws.py check`, both V8 greps, `fisher_greater` for the Fisher thresholds, a count of `plugin/skills` and `plugin/evals`, the before-shell hook on 19 crafted payloads (a JSON payload passed to `python3 ws.py hook before-shell --tool claude-code`), `git config --local --list` on this repository, and three read-only `gh api` GETs for main's protection, rulesets and the Actions token's default. Nothing a guard judges was executed: no push, no `gh` write, and no agent, eval or selection run.

## Verdict

**Holds with fixes.** The core still holds: one literal-word rule in the parser, grading files out of the recording's hash, a review loop with a mark and a gate, and staged eval budgets. Fix findings 1 and 2 before approval. Round 6's fix put the two-line arm into H3's baselines, but no slice teaches `run.py` to run it, and `run.py` is frozen from R3 on, before H3 starts. Criterion 30 and a non-goal forbid any edit to a page after its first attempt, yet H3 plans to cut pages and to move a high-churn page's tables after the baselines. Finding 3 needs a rule before G3 is built, since it decides whether git works in an instance's pinned repositories. The rest can be settled without blocking.

## What survives

Moving them out of the agent-facing hash loses nothing. Verified: `evals/scaffold/` holds `README.md`, `src/` and `tests/` only, and `run.py` passes its own `SETTINGS` with no hooks.
- **The fix-a-bug records are current at the reviewed commit**, as H0a's migration needs. Verified: `python3 ws.py check` exits 0, with only the warning that a plain check has no base commit to compare guided arms against.
- **Criterion 20's anchors are right.** R2's guided arm passes 42 of 45 pooled, 14, 13 and 15, and the anchor at `b754e39` 40 of 45, 14, 11 and 15. Verified, by reading the three records at HEAD and at `b754e39`.
- **R4 can record the guided arm alone.** Slice Z edits `AGENTS.md`, `INDEX.md`, `fix-a-bug.md`, the definition of done and the order service's rule page, which the guided arm alone is given, and the version bump touches `workspace.json` and `wslib/__init__.py`, which no case hashes. Verified, by reading `evals/arms.json`, each case's `add_files` and `covered_files`.
- **Ten pages name M4 in `prove-by`, and each already has a wrapper**, so H3 creates no wrapper and the "no new wrapper" non-goal holds. 28 wrappers name 29 pages. Verified, by the first V8 grep and by listing `plugin/skills`.
- **Every page V8's second grep matches today is named by a slice or left to slice Z's re-run**, and none of the ten H3 pages matches it. Verified, by running both greps.
- **The always-loaded contract has room for slice Z's three lines.** `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and the two pointer files hold 5,157 of 6,000 bytes. Verified, `wc -c`.
- **Push forms the guards already hold.** Abbreviated long options (`--del`, `--dele`, `--force-w`, `--mirr`, `--pru`), `--prune` with every branch, `--atomic -f`, `@:main -f`, and `time`, `nohup` and `env -S` before the push are each refused today. `--force-i` passes, and git documents `--force-if-includes` as a no-op without `--force-with-lease` (inferred). So a workflow pushed on a branch cannot rewrite main with that token unless it asks for write and protection lets it. Verified, by read-only `gh api` GETs; the token's limits are inferred from GitHub's documentation.
