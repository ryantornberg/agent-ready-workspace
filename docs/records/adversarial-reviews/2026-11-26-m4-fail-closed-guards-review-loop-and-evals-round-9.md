# Red team review: docs/plans/m4-fail-closed-guards-review-loop-and-evals.md

A ninth fresh red team's attack on M4's draft hand-off plan, checking the claims its decisions rest on against the repository.

**In short:** holds with fixes, 8 findings. The review gate, `ws.py ready` and the merge policy only work in the workspace's own repository, not in the pinned repositories an instance reviews. A mark is also lost on every CI fix.

The red team read the plan, the eight earlier records and the sources the plan names, at the commit above, with a clean tree. It also read `evals/run.py`'s `prepare`, `agent_command`, `covered` and `plan_runs`, `parts/eval_record.py` in full, `evals/grade.py`'s header and hook loader, every case under `evals/cases/`, `evals/arms.json`, `parts/proof.py`'s docstring, `parts/wrappers.py`'s waiting rules, `parts/plan_budgets.py`, `parts/claim_marks.py`, `parts/stop_gate.py`, `parts/no_admin_merge.py`'s docstring, `wslib/merge.py`'s `merge`, `wslib/runner.py`'s `command_merge`, `command_push`, `push_parts` and `command_commits`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `docs/changing-the-machinery.md`'s path and rules, `docs/playbooks/git-host-operations.md`'s operations table, the review orchestrator's page in full, `docs/playbooks/fix-a-bug.md`'s steps, `docs/size-the-work.md`'s spec process, the hand-off plan template, `INDEX.md`'s chains, `src/README.md` and the manifest's parts. It ran `python3 ws.py check`, `git log` on main for the ten pages, `wc -c` on the always-loaded files, `claude --help`, and one read-only `gh pr list` of merged pull requests. Nothing a guard judges was executed: no push, no `gh` write, and no agent, eval or selection run.

## Verdict

**Holds with fixes.** The core holds: one literal-word rule in the parser, grading files out of the recording's hash, a review loop with a mark and a gate in this repository, and staged eval budgets. But the review loop's claims reach further than its design. Finding 1: the gate, `ws.py ready`, `ws.py merge` and the merge policy are defined only for the workspace's own repository, while an instance's reviews happen in its pinned repositories. Either M4 says so and scopes them, or it defines them there. Finding 2: the mark must exist before CI first runs on three systems, and every later commit loses it, so the per-pull-request estimate and cap are too low. Findings 3 and 4 are two ways the review and teach-back marks can be earned without the work they attest. The rest can be settled without blocking.
