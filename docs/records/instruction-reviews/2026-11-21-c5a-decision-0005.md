# Instruction review: docs/decisions/0005-concurrent-sessions-and-the-edit-ledger.md

Review of what M3 slice C5a changed in decision 0005: the ledger's session field and the stop gate. For the slice's author and agents whose stop is refused.

**In short:** 6 findings: 5 contradiction, 1 trigger. The code and its tests hold; the page's wording of when the gate refuses, and how often, is looser than the code, and the index does not route to it.

In scope: the In short line's new sentence, the edited "What needs a lease" paragraph, the new section "The ledger records each entry's session, and the stop gate reads it", and the new bullet under "What it gave up", each read against the whole page. I read the whole page and its diff against `origin/main`. Beside it I read `AGENTS.md`, `INDEX.md`, decision 0004's "After-edit and stop events", decision 0002, `docs/parts.md`, `docs/layout.md`, `wslib/ledger.py`, `wslib/runner.py` (`stop_event`, `command_stop`, and the before-edit recording in `command_hook`), `wslib/gate.py`, `parts/stop_gate.py` and its cases, `parts/edit_ledger.py`, `parts/page_kinds.py`, the `stop_gate` entry in `workspace.json`, the Stop wiring in `.claude/settings.json` and `.codex/hooks.json`, and `tests/test_after_edit_and_stop.py`. ## Findings

| # | Class | Where | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | contradiction | In short line, and the stop gate paragraph of the new section | The page says a session "is refused its first stop" and that "the stop after it goes ahead", which reads as once per session. Decision 0004 and `docs/parts.md` say a stop is refused "at most once in a row", and `command_stop` keeps no state: it refuses every stop whose event lacks `stop_hook_active`. So while the failure lasts, the first stop of each later turn is refused again. Verified for the runner by reading `command_stop` and decision 0004; that both tools clear `stop_hook_active` on a new turn is inferred, since the recorded runs hold one turn each. | fixed: the In short line says a session cannot finish a turn on its first try, and the stop gate paragraph says a turn is refused at most once and the next turn's first stop is judged again. |
| 2 | contradiction | In short line | "The edit ledger records which session wrote each entry" holds only for edit-tool entries. The new section says an entry `python3 ws.py ledger --add` wrote carries no session, and `ledger._line` writes the quoted path alone when the session is None. Verified by reading the page and `wslib/ledger.py`, and by the passing test `test_an_edit_tool_records_its_session_and_ledger_add_records_none`. | fixed: the In short line says the ledger records which session's edit tool wrote each entry. |
| 3 | contradiction | The new section, the paragraph on the `stop_gate` hook | The page says the stop is refused "while either fails", and that with no entries of its own "the session stops and nothing runs". The code also refuses on a run that times out or cannot start, and refuses a session with no entries when a ledger cannot be read. The `stop_gate` row in `docs/parts.md` names the first two; no page names the third. Verified: `parts/stop_gate.py` refuses any status but `passed`, `stop_event` turns a `LedgerError` into a refusal, and the passing test `test_an_unreadable_ledger_refuses_once_without_running_anything` shows it. | fixed: the paragraph names a run that times out or cannot start, and an edit ledger that cannot be read, which refuses once with nothing run. |

Verified by reading `ledger.read`, which drops the session, and by the passing test `test_each_session_gets_its_own_entry_and_the_commit_check_reads_the_paths`. | fixed: the new section says the commit check reads every entry's path, whoever wrote it.
