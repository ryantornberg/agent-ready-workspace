# Instruction review: docs/playbooks/handoff.md

This records the instruction review of the new handoff playbook, for its author and for agents that stop mid-task or resume from a note.

**In short:** 3 findings: 2 contradiction, 1 step. The page calls the note the whole job while also asking for a plan "Results" entry its steps never make, defers the contract's question-log rule without labelling it an exception, and resumes without naming the working copy or the lease.

## Scope

In scope: the whole page. Read beside it: `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `INDEX.md`, `docs/definition-of-done.md`, `docs/orchestration.md` (with its batch 3 diff), `docs/layout.md`, `docs/glossary.md`, `docs/templates/hand-off-plan.md`, `docs/question-log.md`, `docs/decisions/0005-concurrent-sessions-and-the-edit-ledger.md`, `wslib/ledger.py`, `parts/edit_ledger.py`, the B3 and C5 rows and the C5b split in `docs/plans/m3-reading-layer-machinery-and-kit.md`, the batch 3 diffs of `docs/playbooks/README.md` and `docs/glossary.md`, `docs/playbooks/feedback-walkthrough.md`, and `docs/playbooks/instruction-review.md`. Commands run: `python3 ws.py --help`, `python3 ws.py handoff`, `python3 ws.py rules`, `python3 ws.py check` (exit 0, one unrelated `eval_record` warning).
