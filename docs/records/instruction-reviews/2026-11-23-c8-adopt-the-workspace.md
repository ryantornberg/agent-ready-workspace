# Instruction review: docs/adopt-the-workspace.md

This records the review of slice C8's edits to the adoption how-to, for its author and its readers.

**In short:** 8 findings: 3 step, 3 contradiction, 1 enforcement, 1 trigger; 3 blocking. The first commit of a kit-made instance is refused by `review_trailer`, and no step says how to make it. Step 6 says the eval gates nothing in a new instance, while the instance's contract, copied unchanged, still says a contract change needs a new eval run. Keeping your own copy of a synced page, as the transfer table says, fails the checks for the one proven playbook.

## Scope

In scope: the parts slice C8 changed, read with `git diff docs/adopt-the-workspace.md`: the **In short** line, the section "The kit", the changed rows of "What transfers" and "What does not transfer", steps 1 and 6, the first row of "When an adoption is done", and two rows of "Rules". The rest of the page was read for rows the kit made false.

Read beside it: `AGENTS.md`, `INDEX.md`, `README.md` (with its diff), `workspace.json`, `docs/decisions/0007-the-kit.md`, `docs/decisions/0006-what-proven-means.md`, `docs/changing-the-machinery.md`, `docs/playbooks/instruction-review.md`, `docs/how-tos.md`, `kit/kit.json`, `kit/seed/README.md`, `kit/seed/evals-README.md`, `kit/seed/ci.yml`, `wslib/kit.py`, `wslib/kitlock.py`, `parts/kit_drift.py`, `parts/proof.py`, `parts/eval_record.py`, `evals/run.py`, `tests/test_kit.py`, `.github/workflows/cold-clone.yml`, and in `docs/plans/m3-reading-layer-machinery-and-kit.md` the C8 row, criterion 47 and the entry "The kit's merge setting moves to C8".
