# Instruction review: docs/changing-the-machinery.md

This records the instruction review of M4 slice G1's change to changing the machinery, for its author and anyone pushing or merging machinery.

**In short:** 3 findings, 1 blocking: 2 contradiction (1 blocking), 1 step. The blocking one: step 6 now runs a merge that retargets every open pull request stacked on the merged branch, which git-host operations says always needs a yes first, and no page labels the merge's retarget as an exception.
