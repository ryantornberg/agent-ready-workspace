## Findings

| # | Class | Where | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | contradiction | "Decision", the bullet "After the work ships, the spec is kept as written" | Not blocking. "A later change to the same behavior gets its own plan, which links the old one" has no exception. Size the work's "The gate" sends a change that is small, easy to reverse, in one repository and under a day to fix a bug, with no plan (verified, reading). | 2 | trigger | The title; "Decision", the bullets "While the work is built" and "After the work ships" | Not blocking. The record switches the spec from "changes by dated entries" to "kept as written" when the work "ships". No plan status marks that moment: the plan's `status` runs `draft`, `approved`, `done`, `abandoned` (verified, `docs/layout.md` "Page kinds" and size the work's "When reality diverges"). The record itself says the plan's `status` carries the spec. A plan can be `done` before its release ships, or ship slice by slice, so an agent cannot tell from the plan which rule applies (inferred). | fixed: the switch is the plan's `done` status, in the title, the In short line and the Decision |
