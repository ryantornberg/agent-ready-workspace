# Instruction review: docs/decisions/0006-what-proven-means.md

This records the instruction-file review of decision record 0006, for its author and for agents who mark pages proven or unproven.

**In short:** 6 findings: 1 contradiction, 2 step, 3 enforcement. The proof rules mostly hold, but three are unenforced and two steps leave out the form the check needs.

## Scope

The whole of `docs/decisions/0006-what-proven-means.md` was in scope: the In short line, every section and the Rules table. Read beside it: `AGENTS.md`, `INDEX.md`, `README.md`, `workspace.json`, `docs/layout.md`, `docs/templates/hand-off-plan.md`, `docs/plans/m3-reading-layer-machinery-and-kit.md`, `evals/README.md`, `evals/proofkeys.py`, `evals/run.py` and the front matter of every page under `docs/`. Parts read: `proof`, `page_kinds`, `eval_record`, `direct_push` and `no_admin_merge`, each with its cases file. Commands run: `python3 ws.py rules`, `python3 ws.py check`, and the `proof`, `page_kinds` and `eval_record` parts on small in-memory pages.

| # | Class | Where | Finding | Disposition |

| 1 | contradiction | Decision, Plans name their milestone | It says a plan carries a `milestone` key, then that an approved plan may gain one. | 3 | step | Decision, The marks do not move quietly | The `Demote:` and `Anchor:` lines take a `<page>`. Verified: read `marks` and `approvals` in `parts/proof.py`. | fixed: the Amended paragraph gives a section's form, page path, `#` and the heading's anchor. |
