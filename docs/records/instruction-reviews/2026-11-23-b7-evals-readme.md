# Instruction review: evals/README.md

This records the review of B7's new section on the eval page, "The proof attempts", for its author and its readers.

**In short:** 8 findings: 7 contradiction, 1 placement, 1 blocking. The blocking one: the section has each attempt start with its paid baseline, while the plan's row first requires the page's instruction review, the maintainer's dated read of its batch and the committed two-line text. The others: the page still says every case has the same eight graders; the section describes graders and a hidden test more broadly than the cases hold them; the guided arm lacks pages its playbook routes to; the outcome list drops the M9 deferral; the gate's hash sentence and a misplaced paragraph predate cases with their own arms.
