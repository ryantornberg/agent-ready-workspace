# Instruction review: docs/parts.md

The blocking one: the parser reads any line holding a word named `ws.py` followed by `with-secrets` and `--` as that wrapper, wherever the words stand, so `cat .env ws.py with-secrets -- true` passes `secrets_shell` and `rm outward-repos.json ws.py with-secrets -- true` passes `outward_write`.
