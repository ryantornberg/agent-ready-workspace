# Instruction review: docs/decisions/0004-the-hook-contract.md

This records the review of C5e's edits to decision 0004, the outward-write guard and its two facts, for its author and its readers.

**In short:** 5 findings: 2 enforcement, 2 contradiction, 1 placement. The page says only a person edits the list, but `git checkout`, `git apply`, an interpreter and a write through a symbolic link rewrite it unseen. `git send-pack` pushes past the guard. "A read on the list" means the read list, not the repository list. A `destinations` fact that is unresolved, with its URLs known, passes, an exception the page does not label. The C5e amendment sits under "## Rejected".
