# Instruction review: docs/glossary.md

This records the instruction-file review of the glossary, for its author and for first-time readers who rely on its definitions.

**In short:** 3 findings: 3 contradiction. Three definitions are narrower than what the parts and the eval harness do, so a reader meets things the glossary says cannot exist.

## Scope

The whole of `docs/glossary.md` was in scope: the purpose line, the In short line and every row of its table. Read beside it: `AGENTS.md`, `INDEX.md`, `README.md`, `workspace.json`, `docs/layout.md`, `docs/parts.md`, `docs/decisions/0005-concurrent-sessions-and-the-edit-ledger.md`, `docs/decisions/0006-what-proven-means.md`, `evals/arms.json`, `evals/one-time.json`, `evals/run.py`, `wslib/parts.py` and `wslib/runner.py`. Read the `KIND`, `EVENT` and `PHASES` values of every part under `parts/`.

## Findings

| # | Class | Where | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | contradiction | Table, Hook row | It defines a hook as deciding on one agent tool call before it runs. Three hooks run when a session starts, not on a tool call: `fast_forward`, `stale_pages` and `stale_pins`. Verified: each sets `KIND = "hook"` and `EVENT = "session-start"`. | fixed: the row now covers session events, which only remind. |
| 2 | contradiction | Table, Check row | It says a check reports at the commit and in CI. That is only the default. `direct_push` runs at the push alone, and checks may run in a nightly phase. Verified: `direct_push` sets `PHASES = ("push",)`, and the docstring of `wslib/parts.py` lists the commit, CI, nightly and push phases. | fixed: the row now says the commit and CI are the default phases. |
| 3 | contradiction | Table, Arm row | It names three arms: guided, two-line and bare. The harness also runs one-time arms, such as `routed` in `evals/one-time.json`, and `eval_record` has a case for a one-time arm's record. Verified: read `evals/one-time.json` and the `--one-time` path in `evals/run.py`. | fixed: the row now names one-time arms. |

## Checked with no finding

- step: every path and link the page names exists, including the index's search aliases heading; `python3 ws.py check` passes.
- trigger: the purpose line and the index row for the glossary fire for a reader who meets an unknown word.
- enforcement: the page states no must or never of its own; the machinery rule it mentions is enforced by `direct_push` and stated on its linked page.
- placement: the page holds definitions only, and none of its text sits in the always-loaded layer.
