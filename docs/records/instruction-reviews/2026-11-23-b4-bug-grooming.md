# Instruction review: docs/playbooks/bug-grooming.md

This records the instruction review of slice B4's new bug grooming playbook, a product lane draft, for the change's author.

**In short:** 9 findings: 4 step, 4 contradiction, 1 enforcement; 3 blocking. Step 3 reproduces at `origin/main` with no way to get that tree, so a helper moves a pinned checkout or records a commit it did not measure. Step 7 removes the `effort` label and writes the commit measured without step 6's yes. Step 8's "writes no file" drops the question-log entry. Sizing a bug fits the feature rubric badly.
