# Instruction review: docs/playbooks/adversarial-review.md

This records the instruction review of the new adversarial review playbook, for its author and for agents who run a red team.

**In short:** 6 findings: 2 contradiction, 2 step, 1 trigger, 1 enforcement. The template and the thin-by-design stance hold. The gaps are in who may be the critic, what counts as a finished spec, the `does not hold` path, and one undefined input of the record.

## Findings

| # | Class | Where | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | contradiction | Steps, step 2, against the Rules table | The Rules row says "The critic runs at the driver tier". Step 2 makes the current session the critic whenever it has not written the document, such as a session a person starts only to review. Orchestration's "The binding" says the person chooses that session's model, and the binding governs only what it hands off. So step 2 lets a critic run at any model and effort, an exception the page does not label. Step 2 has no check that the session runs at the driver tier, and no "else dispatch". Verified: read step 2, the Rules row and orchestration's "The binding". | fixed: step 2 makes this session the critic only when it runs at the driver tier's model and effort, and dispatches otherwise; the Rules row now says so. |
| 2 | contradiction | When it runs, and the purpose line, against Steps, step 1 | The page says it fits "a finished spec". The glossary's Spec row and the spec process's "Where it lives" define a spec as three sections of a hand-off plan, agreed before the rest of the plan is written. Step 1's test requires "every section it needs before approval" to be filled, with the default "if you cannot tell that it is finished, it is not". A spec, as this workspace defines it, sits in a draft plan whose other sections are still blank, so it fails that test and step 1 stops. The playbook catalog row repeats "finished spec". Verified: read the glossary row, size-the-work's "Where it lives" and "The four phases", and step 1. | fixed: the page now fits a hand-off plan or a requirements document, and says a spec is reviewed with the plan that holds it, never alone. |
| 3 | step | The record, its front matter | For a document not yet committed, `reviewed-at:` ends `, with the uncommitted change of <whose>`. The critic gets only the template, whose four placeholders are the document path, sources, record path and date. Nothing tells it who the author is, and git records no author for uncommitted changes. So the critic must guess `<whose>`, in a record B7 grades. Verified: read the template's placeholders, step 3's input list and "The record". | fixed: the reviewed-at suffix is now the fixed text ', with the uncommitted change of the author', so the critic has nothing to guess. |
| 4 | step | Steps, steps 5 to 7: the `does not hold` path | The verdict is known at step 4. Step 5 then has the author triage every finding, and an accepted finding comes "with the fix made". Only step 6 says that on `does not hold` you stop and "do not patch the document around it". An agent that follows the steps in order has already patched the document by then. Step 6 also stops before step 7, the only step that commits, so the record of a failed review is left uncommitted in the working copy. No step says to keep it. Verified: read steps 4 to 7 and the closing stop line. | fixed: step 4 now handles a does-not-hold verdict before any triage: fix nothing, commit the record alone and report; step 6 only decides on another round. |
| 5 | trigger | When it runs (route in from the hand-off plan template) | The hand-off plan template is where a plan's author meets the red team. Its "How to use it" step 4 says "Have a fresh session red-team the plan", and its skeleton has a "Red team" table. Neither links this page, so a plan written from the template alone, such as a machinery milestone's plan, never reaches this protocol. Today only the size-the-work ladder, the feature ladder, the catalog, the glossary and the records README route here. Verified: `grep -rn -i adversarial --include='*.md' .` finds no hit in `docs/templates/hand-off-plan.md`. | fixed: step 4 of the hand-off plan template now links this playbook. |
| 6 | enforcement | Steps, step 6, against the Rules table | Step 6 says that on `does not hold` you "stop and report to the person" and "do not patch the document around it". That is a must-not with no row in the Rules table, and the page has no closing line that leaves the rest to prose. Every other must and never on the page has a row. Verified: read step 6 and the Rules table; `python3 ws.py rules` lists eight rows for the page, none for step 6. | fixed: the Rules table has a row for the does-not-hold stop and its never-patch rule. |
