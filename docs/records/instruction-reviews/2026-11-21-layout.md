# Instruction review: docs/layout.md

This records the instruction-file review of the layout page, for its author and for anyone adding, moving or archiving a page.

**In short:** 9 findings: 5 contradiction, 1 enforcement, 2 placement, 1 eval-case. The page's opening rules and archive steps disagree with the `page_kinds` check and other pages, so an agent following it can be refused.

## Scope

The whole of `docs/layout.md` was in scope: every heading, table and list. Read beside it: `AGENTS.md`, `INDEX.md`, `README.md`, `workspace.json`, `docs/glossary.md`, `docs/decisions/0006-what-proven-means.md`, `docs/templates/hand-off-plan.md`, `docs/orchestration.md`, `docs/parts.md`, `archive/README.md`, `.ignore`, and the front matter of every page under `docs/`. Parts read: `page_kinds`, `proof`, `chains`, `pointers`, `loaded_size` and `rules`, each with its cases file, plus `evals/proofkeys.py` and `evals/cases/`. Commands run: `python3 ws.py rules`, `python3 ws.py check`, and `page_kinds` on small in-memory pages.
