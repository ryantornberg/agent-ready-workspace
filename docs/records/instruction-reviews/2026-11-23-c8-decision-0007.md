# Instruction review: docs/decisions/0007-the-kit.md

This records the review of C8's new decision record on the kit, for its author and its readers.

**In short:** 9 findings, 1 blocking: 4 contradiction, 2 step, 1 enforcement, 1 placement, 1 trigger. The blocking one: an instance gets this contract word for word, and it says a change to the contract or the index needs an eval run, which this page says gates nothing there.

## Scope

In scope: the whole page, a new file. Read beside it: `AGENTS.md`, `INDEX.md`, `README.md` and `docs/adopt-the-workspace.md` with their uncommitted changes, `workspace.json`, decisions 0003, 0004 (with its C8 change) and 0006, `docs/changing-the-machinery.md`, `docs/layout.md`, `docs/maintain-the-workspace.md`, `docs/orchestration.md` ("The binding"), `kit/kit.json`, `kit/seed/` (all three files), `wslib/kit.py`, `wslib/kitlock.py`, `wslib/snapshot.py` (`read`), `wslib/runner.py` (`command_kit`), `parts/kit_drift.py`, `parts/proof.py`, `parts/eval_record.py` and `parts/rules.py` with the C8 diff and their cases, `tests/test_kit.py`, and in the M3 plan the C8 row, the Z row and criterion 47.

Runs (all verified):

- In the worktree: `python3 ws.py kit` exits 0 and lists 567 tracked files, 194 synced, 25 seeded and 348 never synced, 35 proof entries and the cases `big-report`, `fix-a-bug` and `nightly-job`. `python3 ws.py check` exits 0. `python3 -m unittest tests.test_kit -b` fails 2 and errors 5 of 23, all because `kit/seed/README.md` and this page are untracked, so `git ls-files` leaves them out. An instance adopted from the worktree fails `check` with six links to this page.
- In a clone under a temporary folder with the C8 change committed on top of 157570e: `tests.test_kit` runs 23 tests OK. `python3 ws.py adopt` makes an instance (195 synced, 25 seeded, 350 never). In it, `selftest` passes, `check` exits 0, and `drift` reports none. A hand edit to `docs/browsers.md` makes `drift` exit 1 and name the file, and `check` fail through `kit_drift`. An edit to `AGENTS.md` passes `drift` and `check --staged`. `upgrade` then refuses, saying nothing changed, and `git status` shows only the two edits.
- Three upgrades from that clone into the instance, after changing the kit: one adds a part with a rules row on a new synced page, one moves that page to `never`, and one moves a synced page's `prove-by` from M6 to M7.

The credited source was opened through the GitHub API. Commit `e540ba7` of `awslabs/aidlc-workflows` exists, and its licence is MIT-0. Its `core/tools/aidlc-distribution.ts` checks hashes of the form `sha256:` and 64 hex digits, and finds a file's hash among known ones before it treats the file as unchanged. Its `scripts/package.ts` handles managed blocks with begin markers.
