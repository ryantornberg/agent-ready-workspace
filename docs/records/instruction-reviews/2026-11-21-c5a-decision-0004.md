# Instruction review: docs/decisions/0004-the-hook-contract.md

This records the instruction review of the C5a edits to decision 0004, for its author and for anyone writing an after-edit or stop hook.

**In short:** 4 findings: 2 contradiction, 1 step, 1 enforcement. The stop report's display is stated as recorded when no run shows it, and the Decision's opening lists still name only the older hooks' needs.

## Scope

In scope: the new "After-edit and stop events" section, the new Amended paragraph for slice C5a, and the new "stop gate costs time" bullet under "What it gave up", read against the whole record. Read beside it: `AGENTS.md`, `INDEX.md`, `README.md`, `docs/parts.md`, `docs/decisions/0005-concurrent-sessions-and-the-edit-ledger.md`, `wslib/events.py`, `wslib/runner.py` (`index_fact`, `command_after_edit`, `stop_event`, `command_stop`), `wslib/gate.py`, `wslib/ledger.py`, `wslib/parts.py`, `parts/chain_reminder.py`, `parts/stop_gate.py` and their cases files, `.claude/settings.json`, `.codex/hooks.json`, `tests/records/after-edit-and-stop.json` and `tests/test_after_edit_and_stop.py`. Ran `python3 -m unittest discover -b tests -p 'test_after_edit_and_stop.py'` (24 tests, OK) and `python3 ws.py selftest` (all parts ok). The gate's 18-second deadline and the 20-second wait under "What it gave up" were checked and both hold: `SECONDS` in `wslib/gate.py` leaves the rest of 20 seconds for the hook's start and reply, and the recorded-stops test asserts an answer under 20 seconds.

## Findings

| # | Class | Where | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | contradiction | After-edit and stop events, the `stop` bullet | The page says that on a stop with `stop_hook_active` set the runner's report goes out as a `systemMessage` "shown to the person", and opens the section saying the recorded run shows what both tools send; but in that run the capture hook replied nothing on the second stop, so no run shows either tool displaying a `systemMessage`, and the README's Codex limits say the report's display is inferred, not recorded. Verified: `capture_hook_replied` is `null` on both tools' second `Stop` call in `tests/records/after-edit-and-stop.json`; a search finds `systemMessage` only in `wslib/events.py`, tests, this page and no record; the README's Codex bullet reads "the report's display is inferred, not recorded". | fixed: the `stop` bullet says the block's display was recorded for both tools, and that the report goes back as `systemMessage` with its display inferred, not recorded. |
| 2 | contradiction | Decision, "What a part may read" and "Where the engine does the rest" | These lists say a session hook and a before-shell hook may declare `NEEDS` and that the engine works through git and the snapshot; the new section gives after-edit and stop hooks needs of their own, and the C5a Amended paragraph says the gate's runs are the engine's, in `wslib/gate.py`, yet neither list names them, so the exception is not labelled where a reader first learns what a hook may read. Verified: read both lists and `NEEDS` in `wslib/parts.py`, which allows `"index"` on `after-edit` and `"own_edits"` and `"gate"` on `stop`. Inferred: `stop` is not a "session hook" in the lists' sense, since `wslib/parts.py` keeps it out of `SESSION_EVENTS`. The list's omission of before-edit's needs (`"lease"`, `"rule_pages"`) predates this change. | fixed: both lists gain a bullet, each marked as amended by slice C5a: after-edit and stop hooks may declare needs, and the gate's runs are the engine's, in `wslib/gate.py`. The missing before-edit needs predate this change and are left. |
| 3 | step | After-edit and stop events, the needs bullets | The page defines `"own_edits"` as each working copy's paths and `"gate"` as a list of runs with four statuses, but when an edit ledger cannot be read the runner gives `"own_edits"` one placeholder entry, `working_copy` null and the path `(an edit ledger that cannot be read)`, and `"gate"` as `None`; a hook written to the page would treat that as a real path or iterate `None`. Likewise an after-edit hook whose config names no index is given `INDEX.md`, a default the page does not state. Verified: `stop_event` and `index_fact` in `wslib/runner.py`; `test_an_unreadable_ledger_refuses_once_without_running_anything` passed; `parts/stop_gate.py` guards with `isinstance(runs, list)`, a guard the page never tells a hook author to write. | fixed: the needs bullets state the default index, `None` for an index that cannot be read, and what `own_edits` and `gate` hold when a ledger cannot be read. |
| 4 | enforcement | After-edit and stop events, the `after-edit` bullet | "A hook on it must set `MODE = \"remind\"`" names no enforcement, though one exists: loading a part refuses an after-edit hook that does not remind. Verified: `load` in `wslib/parts.py` raises for `AFTER_EVENTS` with any other mode, and `test_an_after_edit_hook_must_remind` passed. | fixed: the `after-edit` bullet says loading a part refuses any other mode. |
