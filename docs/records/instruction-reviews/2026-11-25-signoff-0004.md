# Instruction review: docs/decisions/0004-the-hook-contract.md

This records the instruction review of the M3 sign-off's amendment to decision 0004, for its author and the readers of the hook contract.

**In short:** 4 findings, 2 blocking: 2 enforcement (both blocking), 2 contradiction. The blocking ones: a push word zsh expands by glob grouping or a glob qualifier still passes `no_force_push`, and a qualifier runs a hidden `git push -f origin main`; and `git remote-https` reads a forced push of main from its input and passes every guard, so refusing `send-pack` and `http-push` does not close the route of pushing without `git push`.
