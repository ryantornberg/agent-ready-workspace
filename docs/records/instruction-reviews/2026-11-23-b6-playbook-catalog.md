# Instruction review: docs/playbooks/README.md

This records the review of B6's edits to the playbook catalog on the wrappers, for their author and the catalog's readers.

**In short:** 6 findings: 1 placement, 2 trigger, 2 step, 1 enforcement; 1 blocking. The new rules row links a heading of `evals/README.md` that an instance's eval page, made from the kit's seed, does not have, so `python3 ws.py check` fails in every instance the kit makes. The one step that makes Claude Code load the wrappers sits on this page, not with the README's setup steps, and nothing on this machine has run it. The instruction review's row still leaves out guidance pages, and the index has no route to wrappers. "Core" is never defined. The lane-draft rule says "until proven", while its check refuses a lane page's wrapper for good.

## Scope

In scope: in "The catalog", the "At the commit" row; in "Three ways to follow a playbook", item 3; in "Rules", the changed lane-draft row and the four new rows.
