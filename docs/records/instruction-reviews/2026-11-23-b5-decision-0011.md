# Instruction review: docs/decisions/0011-pages-go-straight-to-main.md

This records the review of B5's new decision record on pages going straight to main, for its author and its readers.

**In short:** 3 findings, 2 blocking: 2 contradiction, 1 enforcement. The blocking ones: "pages go straight to main" leaves out two page changes that may not, a proof anchor and a page the eval covers. It also leaves out that in a team's workspace a person, not the agent, pushes pages.

## Findings

| # | Class | Where | Finding | Disposition |

| 1 | contradiction | The **In short** line, and "Decision", the bullet "Pages go straight to main" | Blocking. An agent following this page commits such a change on main and pushes it, and the commit check or the pre-push hook refuses it. | fixed: a "Two exceptions" bullet names a page the eval covers and a proof anchor, and the In short line names both |
| 2 | contradiction | The **In short** line, and "Decision", the bullet "Pages go straight to main" | Blocking.
