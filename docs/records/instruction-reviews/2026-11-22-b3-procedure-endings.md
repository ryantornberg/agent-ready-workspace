# Instruction review: docs/procedure-endings.md

This records the instruction review of the new procedure endings page, for its author and for anyone writing a page that links it.

**In short:** 11 findings, 2 of them blocking: 3 contradiction, 4 step, 1 trigger, 1 enforcement, 1 placement, 1 eval-case. The blocking two: the question-log step commits another session's uncommitted rows, and no commit or push path covers a code change.
